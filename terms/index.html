1. What Mate Wallet is, and what it is not

Mate Wallet is a non-custodial software wallet for a single asset: USDT on the TON blockchain. It is a tool that stores keys on your device and helps you sign and broadcast blockchain transactions.

Mate Wallet is not a bank, not a bank account, not a custodian, not a money transmitter, not an exchange, and not a financial product. We do not hold your funds. We do not hold, escrow, or have access to your private keys or your recovery phrase at any time. There is no account with us, no deposit with us, and no balance owed to you by us.

The app does not offer exchange of one asset for another, does not offer buying or selling for fiat currency, does not offer lending, staking, yield, or any form of investment return, and does not perform identity verification (KYC) or hold funds on your behalf.

1.1 Amounts shown in US dollars

Amounts shown in US dollars are an informational equivalent of your USDT balance. USDT is a token issued by a third party; it is not a US dollar, not a deposit, and not a financial product. Mate Wallet is not a bank account.

USDT (Tether) is issued and administered by a third party with which we have no relationship and over which we have no control. Its value, redeemability, and continued existence are entirely outside our control. USDT may trade above or below one US dollar.

The app counts one USDT as one US dollar. The figure shown with a $ sign is your USDT amount, displayed in dollars at a fixed rate of 1:1. It is not a market conversion and is not updated from any exchange rate. If USDT trades above or below one dollar, the figure shown will not reflect that difference, and the amount you can obtain for your USDT elsewhere may be more or less than the figure displayed.

The amount actually transferred, and the amount actually recorded on the blockchain, is always the USDT amount.

2. Your keys, your responsibility

When you create a wallet, the app generates a 24-word recovery phrase on your device. That phrase, and the private key derived from it, are the only means of controlling your funds.

  • The recovery phrase is stored on your device and, if you enable backup, in your own iCloud Keychain (see §5). We never receive it, never transmit it, and cannot reproduce it.
  • If you lose your recovery phrase, no one can restore your wallet — not even us. There is no password reset, no account recovery, and no support procedure that can return access to you. Your funds will be permanently inaccessible.
  • If someone else obtains your recovery phrase, they obtain full and irrevocable control of your funds. We cannot freeze, reverse, or block anything.

You are solely responsible for keeping your recovery phrase secret and for keeping at least one copy of it that you control.

3. Transfers are final

Blockchain transfers are irreversible. Once a transfer is broadcast and confirmed, it cannot be cancelled, recalled, reversed, or refunded by us, by the network, or by anyone else.

You are solely responsible for verifying, before you confirm a transfer:

  • the recipient address — an address that is mistyped, pasted from the wrong source, substituted by malware, or belongs to a different blockchain network will result in permanent loss of the funds sent;
  • the amount;
  • whether the recipient requires a comment / memo / tag. Exchanges commonly credit deposits by memo. A transfer that is technically successful but lacks the memo the recipient requires may not be credited, and recovering it is a matter between you and that recipient, not between you and us.

Mate Wallet transfers USDT on the TON network only. Sending to an address of another network (for example Ethereum or TRON) will result in permanent loss.

4. Transactions are public

Every transfer you make is recorded permanently on the public TON blockchain. Your wallet address, the amounts, the counterparty addresses, the timestamps and any comment you attach are publicly visible to anyone, forever, and cannot be deleted by us or by you. Anyone who learns that an address belongs to you can see its entire history.

5. Backup to iCloud Keychain

Backup is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, a copy of your recovery phrase is written to your own iCloud Keychain, which is operated by Apple under your Apple ID. We do not receive it, do not store it, and have no access to it.

You should understand the following before enabling it:

  • The backup copy is not protected by Face ID or Touch ID. A synchronized iCloud Keychain item cannot carry a biometric access control list; this is a limitation imposed by Apple, not a choice we made. The copy is readable while your device is unlocked. (The copy kept locally on your device, by contrast, is protected by biometrics or your device passcode.)
  • Anyone who gains control of your Apple ID, or of an unlocked device signed in to it, may be able to read the backup copy and therefore control your funds.
  • Deleting the item on one device deletes it everywhere that Apple ID syncs.
  • If iCloud Keychain is disabled on your device, no backup exists, regardless of anything the interface may have shown you in the past.

Backup is a convenience. It does not replace writing your 24 words down and keeping them somewhere you control.
