Short version

Mate Wallet works without an account. There is no registration, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier and no tracking of any kind. Your recovery phrase and private keys never leave your device except into your own iCloud Keychain, if you choose to enable backup.

By default we receive nothing about you. There is one exception, and it is optional and off by default: if you turn on notifications, the app sends your wallet address and your iPhone's notification token to Mate's notification server so that we can tell you when you receive money (see §4).

The app also talks to one third party — TonAPI — because a wallet cannot read a blockchain without a node. What that involves is set out below in full.

1. What we collect

Unless you turn on notifications, Mate App LLC receives no data from the app. Whether or not notifications are on, we do not collect, store, process, sell or share:

  • your name, e-mail address, phone number or any identity information;
  • your recovery phrase, private keys or passwords;
  • your balance, transactions or contacts;
  • your advertising identifier;
  • usage analytics, behavioural events or crash reports.

If you turn on notifications, our notification server stores exactly two things: your wallet address and your iPhone's notification token. Nothing else. Details are in §4.

There is no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK in the application. There is no advertising framework. We do not use cookies or any similar technology in the app. We do not use any data to track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share data with data brokers.

3. If you turn on iCloud backup

Backup is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, a copy of your recovery phrase is written to your own iCloud Keychain, operated by Apple under your Apple ID.

  • It goes to Apple, not to us. We never receive it and have no access to it. Apple's handling of it is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.
  • It syncs to the other devices signed in to the same Apple ID.
  • It is not protected by Face ID or Touch ID. A synchronized iCloud Keychain item cannot carry a biometric access control list — this is a limitation imposed by Apple's Keychain, not a choice we made. While your device is unlocked, the copy is readable. The local working copy on your device is biometrically protected; the iCloud copy is not.
  • Turning backup off removes the copy from iCloud across your devices.

5. What the app sends to TonAPI, and why

A wallet cannot see a blockchain without asking a node. Mate Wallet asks one provider: TonAPI (tonapi.io), operated by TON Apps Group. This is a third party; we do not control it, and its handling of the requests it receives is governed by its own privacy policy and terms, not by this one.

For blockchain data the application makes requests to TonAPI only. The only other host the app's own code contacts is Mate's notification server, and only if you turn notifications on (§4).

As with any internet request, the provider also necessarily sees your IP address and the fact and timing of the request. Taken together, this allows the provider — and anyone with lawful access to its records — to associate your IP address with your wallet address and your activity. We have no insight into what they retain.

Requests carry an API key identifying the application, not you. Your recovery phrase and private keys are never sent to TonAPI or anywhere else. Signing happens entirely on your device; only the already-signed result is transmitted.

If you prefer not to expose your IP address to the provider, use a VPN or a similar measure at the device level. The app has no setting for this.

6. The blockchain is public and permanent

Every transfer you make is written permanently to the public TON blockchain. Your address, the counterparty address, the amount, the time and any comment you attach are visible to anyone, forever. Neither you nor we can delete or alter them. Anyone who learns that an address belongs to you can see its entire history.

This is a property of the blockchain itself, not of this application.

7. Children

Mate Wallet is not directed to children. The Terms of Use require you to be at least 18, or the age of majority where you live if that is higher. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Apart from the optional notification record described in §4, there is nothing for a parent or guardian to ask us to delete; anything on the device is removed by deleting the app.

8. Your rights

Data-protection rights such as access, correction, portability and erasure apply to personal data held by a controller. The only data we can hold about you is the optional notification record (§4) — you can delete it yourself by turning notifications off, or ask us to. Beyond that:

  • data on your device is removed by deleting the app;
  • data in your iCloud Keychain is managed by you, through Apple, under your Apple ID;
  • data on the blockchain cannot be removed by anyone, including us — that is a property of a blockchain, not a policy choice;
  • the provider that serves blockchain data to the app is a separate company with its own policy; requests about what it retains go to it, not to us.

If you believe we nevertheless hold personal information about you, write to us at the address in §10 and we will tell you what we find. We expect the answer to be "nothing" or "the notification record", and we would rather say so to you directly than have you wonder.

On the GDPR specifically. Mate Wallet is not offered in the European Union or the European Economic Area — see the Terms of Use, §11. This policy therefore does not set out a GDPR lawful basis, a representative or a supervisory authority. If that territory decision changes, this section has to be rewritten before the app ships there, not afterwards.
